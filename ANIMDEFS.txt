// THE MIRROR'S SURFACE.

// A camera texture: the scene rendered from a viewpoint actor straight onto a model's skin.
// That is the whole trick behind being able to look at yourself.
//
// 3:2, the shape of the 3 m x 2 m panel (vr_mirror_width / _height), so you are not squashed.

// so it is a tool you switch on to check something and switch off again, never something left
// running.

	fit 768 512
