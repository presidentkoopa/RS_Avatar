## The Depot and the avatar hands (decided 2026-10-28)

The Depot (`main`, 483f767 and later) has per-gun FBX hand poses for all 30 BD22 guns, as data. Each one
holds:
- the finger shapes, trigger and squeeze;
- where the hand sits on the gun;
- the support hand;
- the reload holds.

The tools that made them come with it. It is the **same hand skeleton** REMA's poses come from
(`hand_final.fbx`).

Today REMA has about eight shared poses, one per gun type. The work is to give REMA the Depot's per-gun
poses, on each avatar's own hands. The blockers and the order are in `docs/plans/REMA_BLOCKERS.md` (a copy
of the Depot's `docs/handbook/REMA_BLOCKERS.md`). The owner decided:
- REMA's held guns move to the **animated originals**. The single-frame `_wm` meshes are retired.
- The weapon card carries the Depot's **full measured placement** (rotation and offset), not palm and rake
  with a roll added.
- **First proof: Pistol and Rifle**, on c_doom_marine, with full poses (gun hand and support) and the
  reloads.
- **Each avatar's own hand** is fitted from the Depot pose. The Depot's hand meshes are never drawn on a body.

**Not started: the owner said wait for him.** The first job when he says go: measure how a hand sits on the
gun from the Depot's pose. That replaces REMA's identity `[GUNANCHOR]` guess, which is almost certainly why
every gun hand pose looked wrong.
