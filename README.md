# RS_Avatar

The VR body. RS_Avatar draws the player a real torso-up avatar in the world --
head, spine, arms and legs -- and keeps it consistent with where the headset and
the controllers actually are, rather than with where a viewmodel pretends they
are. The ZScript side lives in `zscript/avatar/`: the body actor itself, the
body set (`bodies.zs`), the arm/leg solving and the mirror pass, plus a fit mode
for calibrating a body to the player's own proportions. `genbodies.py` builds the
body models and `pack.py` packs the folder into `RS_Avatar.pk3`.

It needs the UZDXREMA engine fork; the bone reads, forced model angles and body
hooks it drives are not in stock GZDoom.

## Body holsters

The body holsters live here too: six gun holsters (hips, chest, shoulders), an ammo pouch and a
grenade spot. See `HOLSTERS.md`. The engine core is UZDXREMA's `p_vrtorso.cpp` and `p_vrgear.cpp`;
this repo holds the table (`VRGEAR.txt`), the rules (`zscript/gear/gear.zs`), what you see, feel
and hear (`zscript/gear/look.zs`, `MODELDEF.gear`, `RAGFIT.txt`).

The older RS_Holsters system (merged here on 2026-10-27) was retired on 2026-11-09. Its files are
in `reference/retired_holsters/`; they are not packed and not loaded. Do not load an old
`RS_Holsters` pk3 beside this one.

## Gun in hand

`VRHANDS.rsq` and `VRHANDPOSE.rsq` tell the engine where RSQ BD Reloaded's own hand sits on each
gun and how its fingers are posed (113 frames), so the avatar's hand takes that place. Both are
generated by UZDXREMA's `tools/avatar/gen_avatarhands.py` and `gen_modhand_poses.py`. Do not edit
them by hand.
