How the points were found

  • Palm: where c_doom_marine's grip socket goes.On the handle's centre line, at the height where that body's middle finger sits under the guard and its index pad sits on the card's trigger point. Both come from the body's measured hand (palm width 2.03 map units), so the hand closes on the grip instead of the wrist landing there.
  • Rake: the handle's own tilt.The centre line of the stretch the three lower fingers close on, cut square to itself until it stops turning.
  • Support: the middle of what the off hand closes on.Your click's position along the gun is kept; the point moves into the centre of the forend. A forend deeper than 5.5 cm is cupped from below instead. Pistols use Ermac's two-hand hold, sized to the body. Vertical grips and held magazines are treated like a second pistol grip.

Ermac's posed hands are drawn on each sheet only to show the hold. They are not used in the engine or the packs. Guns that share a mesh get the same numbers.
